// how it works

Three steps. No laptop.

1

Make a sound

Record it, grab it from a video, or find a free one on the web, right inside the app. Trim it to the good part and give it a name.

2

Plug in the drive

Take the car's USB drive out of the glovebox, plug it into your phone and tap Lock Chime. The app puts it exactly where the car looks and double-checks it landed.

3

Lock the car

Put the drive back, flip one switch on the car's screen, and lock it from outside. That's your sound.

Want the details, the folder names and the gotchas? They're all in Help.

// made to be played with

An app you'll open just to poke the lock.

Most car utilities feel like a settings screen. This one feels like a toy that happens to do the job.

  • A real 3D padlock you can spin, flick and pop open. It clunks shut when you install a sound.
  • Sounds and haptics for every tap. The dial clicks as it turns.
  • Four looks: Midnight, Paper, Electric and Hazard, each with a matching app icon. Try them with the lock button in the corner.
  • People: everyone in the family gets their own favorites, in their own order.
  • Share any sound by Messages or AirDrop. Friends with the app import it in a tap.
