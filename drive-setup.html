Drive setup

One USB drive for TeslaCam and Boombox.

The car ignores a Boombox folder on the drive that records TeslaCam. But it reads every partition on a drive as its own volume. So split the drive once: a small Boombox part, a big TeslaCam part. Both work, nothing to unplug, and the Lock Chimes app sees each part as a drive.

After that

Back in the car

  • Boombox sounds go in the Boombox folder on the BOOMBOX part. Up to 5, no spaces in the names. The app does this for you.
  • The lock chime is LockChime.wav at the top level. The app writes it to whichever part you pick; putting it on both is fine.
  • TeslaCam records to the TESLACAM part exactly as before.
  • On the iPhone, the drive shows up as two drives (BOOMBOX and TESLACAM). Pick BOOMBOX for Boombox installs.

Prefer not to use the Terminal? A small Mac app that does all of this with one click is on the way. In the meantime a second USB stick in a glovebox hub works too.
