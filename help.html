Install a lock chime

1

Make a sound

Record anything, pull the audio out of a video in Photos, open a file, or tap a download button on a site with free sounds inside the app's Find Sounds browser. Trim to the best 5 seconds, boost, fade, name it.

2

Plug in the drive

Take the car's USB drive out of the glovebox port and plug it into your phone (USB-C, or Lightning with a camera adapter on older iPhones). Tap Lock Chime and pick the drive when the file picker opens. The app writes LockChime.wav at the top level of the drive, forces it onto the drive, and reads it back to confirm.

3

Lock the car

Drive back in the glovebox. On the car's screen: Toybox → Boombox → Lock Sound → USB. Lock the car from outside. That's your sound.

  • Boombox (Pro): up to 5 sounds in a Boombox folder, WAV or MP3, under 1 MB each, no spaces in the names. The app renames them for you. They show up as "USB <name>" in Toybox → Boombox. Needs its own USB stick, or a drive split into two parts.
  • Drive check: format, free space, what's on the drive, and warnings when something's off.
  • History: every install is kept, so you can put an old favorite back in one tap.
  • Drives: each physical drive is remembered separately, even identical models. Pick a new one once.
// things we learned the hard way

FAQ

Tested on a real car. Email support@faradaysoft.com if yours does something different.

The car ignores my LockChime.wav

The lock chime must be a WAV named exactly LockChime.wav at the top level of the drive, not inside a folder. Several guides say Boombox/LockChime.wav; on our test car that file was ignored. The app writes it to the top level for you. Also check Toybox → Boombox → Lock Sound is set to USB.

My Boombox sounds don't show up

Boombox needs its own USB stick. On a drive that also records TeslaCam the car ignores the Boombox folder entirely (tested; Tesla's manual says the drive "can only contain one folder"). Use a spare exFAT stick with nothing but Boombox on it, or split one drive into a TeslaCam part and a Boombox part, and the sounds appear in Toybox → Boombox as "USB <name>". WAV and MP3 both play. File names can't have spaces; the app handles that. Boombox plays while parked.

Can I keep TeslaCam and Boombox on one drive?

Yes, with two partitions: a small BOOMBOX one and a big TESLACAM one. The car reads each as its own drive, and so does your phone. This page writes the Mac or Windows commands for you. A Mac app that does it in one click is coming.

On Android, the drive picker shows my phone's folders instead of the drive

Android only lets an app write to a drive you pick yourself. When the picker opens, tap the menu in the top-left corner, choose the USB drive by name, then tap Use this folder at the bottom. You only do it once per drive. If the drive isn't listed, unplug it and plug it back in; the phone takes a second to mount it.

The app can't see my USB drive

iOS shows USB drives in the Files app under Locations. If it isn't there, the phone isn't reading it: usually an NTFS-formatted drive (use exFAT or FAT32) or a drive that needs more power than the phone's port provides (use a powered hub). Each physical drive is remembered separately, even identical ones; pick a new drive once in the app.

Which cars work?

Teslas with the external speaker: Model 3 built from late 2019, all Model Y, Model S and X from 2021, and Cybertruck. If Toybox → Boombox exists on your screen, you're set.

How long can a sound be?

The car plays up to 5 seconds for the lock chime and wants the file under 1 MB. The app trims and converts so you don't have to think about it.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. No accounts, no analytics, no server of ours. Sounds stay on your phone (on iPhone, visible in Files under On My iPhone › Lock Chimes). Full details in the privacy policy.

Can I use songs or movie clips?

Use sounds you have the right to use. The app ships with original chimes and makes it easy to record your own; it hosts no third-party clips. Sites with free sounds state their own terms, and many allow personal use in your own car.
